FAFSA and Aid Renewal:
- FAFSA Deadline: Continuing students are recommended to complete the FAFSA annually by January 1.
- Duration Limit: Beloit College scholarships and grants are locked in for a maximum of eight (8) semesters.
- Academic Performance: Academic performance and course completion are evaluated at the end of each semester to maintain federal, state, and institutional aid.
- Course Withdrawal: Withdrawing while a term is in session may result in losing a portion of financial aid.

Enrollment Levels for Financial Aid:
- Full-time: 3.0+ units
- Three-quarter time: 2.25 - 2.99 units
- Half-time: 1.5 - 2.24 units
- Less than half-time: 0.01 - 1.49 units
