Resolving Accounting Holds A student may resolve an Accounting/Enrollment hold by contacting the Student Accounts Office to determine the nature of the hold and the actions required to address it. Required actions may include, but are not limited to: ● Paying the outstanding balance in full ● Securing sufficient outside funding to pay the outstanding balance in full ● Establishing an approved payment plan ● Completing outstanding financial documents (if applicable) Students Already Living on Campus Students who are already living in the Residence Halls due to summer housing or co-curricular activities must clear an Accounting/Enrollment hold no later than one week before the first day of classes. Students who do not meet this deadline will be required to move out of the Residence Halls and may not attend classes or participate in co-curricular activities. In some cases, the Student Accounts Office may provide written authorization for a student to live or remain in campus housing while following an approved action plan. Participation in co-curricular activities may be approved in the action plan, depending on the unique situation. The student must receive this permission before moving into or continuing to live in the Residence Halls. International Students Failure to enroll in classes may negatively impact international students’ status in the United States. For more information on how this affects their status, please contact the International Student Advisor. Other Enrollment Holds Students may have other enrollment holds for reasons unrelated to an outstanding balance. Students with these holds must contact the Dean of Students Office and develop an action plan based on the nature of the hold. H. Mail Center The mail center serves two primary functions: (1) as a central mail distribution center for the U.S./International mail and packages delivered to campus. As such, regular postal rules apply to all stamped mail. Any questionable stamped mail will follow the U.S. postal procedures for follow up inquiry; (2) as a College service for inter-campus mail. Incidents reported to mail center staﬀ or by mail center staﬀ will follow the normal campus investigation/disciplinary process. The Campus Mail Center also retains the right to add additional policies and procedures, given the nature of our on-campus facility and staﬃng. Students are to make sure that mail and packages include their proper name and their student box number to ensure that their mail and packages are delivered in a timely manner and to the correct person. 75
