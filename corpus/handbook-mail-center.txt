The mail center serves two primary functions: (1) as a central mail distribution center for the U.S./International mail and packages delivered to campus. As such, regular postal rules apply to all stamped mail. Any questionable stamped mail will follow the U.S. postal procedures for follow up inquiry; (2) as a College service for inter-campus mail. Incidents reported to mail center staff or by mail center staff will follow the normal campus investigation/disciplinary process.

The Campus Mail Center also retains the right to add additional policies and procedures, given the nature of our on-campus facility and staffing. Students are to make sure that mail and packages include their proper name and their student box number to ensure that their mail and packages are delivered in a timely manner and to the correct person.
