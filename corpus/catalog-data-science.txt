Data Science Major Requirements (14.5-15 units)

1. Data Science and Data Analytics 210 (.5), 345, and 385.
2. Four units in mathematics: Mathematics 110, 115, 205, and 275.
3. Three units in computer science: Computer Science 111, 204, and 367.
4. At least 1.5 units in data science and data analytics or humanities electives: chosen from Art 325, Data Analytics and Science 215, English 265, Greek, Latin, and Ancient Mediterranean Studies 249, Media Studies 150, Writing 215.
5. Disciplinary specialty: 3 units in one approved discipline (e.g., Anthropology, Biology, Chemistry, Economics, Education, Environmental Studies, Geology, Health & Society, Physics, Political Science, Psychology, Sociology, or Sports Analytics).
6. Data Science and Data Analytics 310 (.5).

Double Counting Policy:
If students majoring in Data Science or Data Analytics want to major or minor in another discipline, up to four units are allowed to be double counted.

Departmental Honors in Data Science & Data Analytics:
- Academic Requirement: Cumulative GPA of at least 3.5 and DSDA major GPA of at least 3.5.
- Application Deadline: Written submission to DSDA chair no later than the last day to add one-unit courses in the final term.
- Project & Presentation: Original research or thorough exposition presented publicly on- or off-campus.
