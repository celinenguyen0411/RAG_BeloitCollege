Economics Major Requirements (10-10.5 units)

Required Core Courses:
1. Economics 199 (Introductory Economics).
2. Economics 211 (Intermediate Microeconomics) and Economics 251 (Intermediate Macroeconomics).
3. Economics 202 (Econometrics and Data Analysis) or Mathematics 106.
4. Electives: Four elective units in Economics at the 200-level or 300-level, with at least two at the 300-level.
5. Senior Capstone: Economics 380 or Senior Thesis (ECON 390).

Major Policy Rules:
- GPA Minimum: A cumulative GPA of at least 2.000 in all major courses is required for graduation.
- Transfer Credit Limit: A maximum of 2 transfer units may be applied toward the Economics major with departmental permission.
