Biochemistry Major Requirements (12.5-13 units)

Required Courses:
1. Biology: BIOL 111, BIOL 205, and BIOL 289.
2. Chemistry: CHEM 117 (or 118), CHEM 220, CHEM 230, CHEM 235, and CHEM 300.
3. Biochemistry: BIOC 260, BIOC 360, and BIOC 380.
4. Mathematics & Physics: MATH 110 and PHYS 101 (or PHYS 102).

Degree Options & Capstone:
- Bachelor of Science (BS) in Biochemistry: Requires an additional 4 units in natural sciences, mathematics, or computer science outside the Biochemistry program.
- Capstone Requirement: Must be fulfilled via a Senior Thesis or approved Capstone project.
