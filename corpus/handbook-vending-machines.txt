All members of the residential community must share responsibility for security. Residents are issued a key or key card for the exterior doors to their building. Key cards normally allow access from 10:00 a.m. – 10:00 p.m. After that time, only the building assignment card will open the doors. These hours are subject to change. Room Safety : When someone knocks on your door, ask who it is and check the person, using the door viewer provided, before responding. You should keep your room door locked at all times. Each student is encouraged to record the serial numbers of all valuable items. It is also advisable to make special arrangements for the protection of valuables when absent for extended periods. The protection of personal property is the individual’s responsibility. For additional information and policies regarding safety and security go here . Smoking/Vaping: Smoking and vaping are not allowed in any building on campus, including residence halls. See the Smoking/Vaping policy for additional details. Solicitation: Salespersons are prohibited from selling in residence halls and from door-to-door solicitation unless they have written authorization from the Director of Residential Life. No one is to place ﬂyers on cars or mark cars parked on campus in any way. Space Heaters: Space heaters are not allowed in residence spaces due to the ﬁre danger they present. If your room is too cold please ﬁll out a work order (Asset Essentials) request via the Portal or contact your RA for assistance. Storage: Storage areas for student belongings are available only in Whitney and Porter Hall and for those students that are either international students, live more than 1,000 miles away, or are going abroad for the semester. Storage is on a ﬁrst-come, ﬁrst-served basis. Students are allowed to store up to ﬁve bins and all must be clearly labeled with their name, class year and box number. Vending Machines: Vending machines are conveniently located in several residence halls. Additionally, more machines are available in the Powerhouse,the lower level of Pearsons Hall, and on the ﬁrst ﬂoor of Morse Library Problems with the equipment, including refunds, should be reported using the contact information listed on the machine. Weapons and Explosives: The use and/or storage of any weapons, ﬁrearms, ammunition, or explosives of any kind in the residence halls are strictly prohibited.
