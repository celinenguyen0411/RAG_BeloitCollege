Room Changes/Switching Rooms/Changing Roommates: Any room changes must be approved by the Residence Life Oﬃce . For roommate or ﬂoor mate conﬂicts, the process begins by speaking with your roommate or ﬂoor mate. If it cannot be worked out, you must then speak with your Resident Assistant (RA) to facilitate mediation. If resolution is still not made, then you are to speak with your Residence Life Coordinator (RLC). If the situation still isn’t resolved, then a room change may occur if there is space available. There are times where an immediate room change may need to happen for various reasons; those are determined by the Oﬃce of Residence Life or the Dean of Students. Room Inventory and Damages: At the beginning of a student’s occupancy of a room, an inventory of the condition of the room and its furnishings will be made by the student and the resident assistant. Blank inventory forms are available through the RA and/or the Residence LifeOﬃce. Within the ﬁrst week of occupancy, students are to inventory the condition of their room in detail, being very speciﬁc regarding any damages present upon moving into the room. Upon checkout, the room condition will be compared to the initial inventory. Charges for damage done to the room and/or missing or broken furniture will be assessed. Those who do not submit an initial inventory should know that the room will be expected to be in perfect condition upon check-out, and there is no opportunity to appeal charges without an initial inventory. Failure to complete and return a room inventory when moving into the room will result in a charge to the student. Room Personalization: Students are strongly encouraged to personalize their rooms. This can be accomplished in several ways: 1. Moving and arranging the furniture to ﬁt their style and living needs. (All furniture is kept free standing and moveable for this reason.) 2. Decorate the walls with posters, pictures, etc. 3. Bring plants in to add color and a sense of home to the room. Remember to keep in mind the policies under “Room Alterations” above. Safety and Security: Every attempt is made to provide safe, secure buildings. All members of the residence community must share responsibility for security. All residents can help in this process by: reporting suspicious strangers to Security, keeping ﬁre doors closed, not propping open outer doors when they are locked, and keeping room doors locked.
