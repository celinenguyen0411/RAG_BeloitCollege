No pets, other than fish in an aquarium (5-gallon tank, maximum), are allowed to live on campus with students. Residents having pets that live with them on campus may be required to have the pet removed from campus and/or be required to move off-campus (and charged the full room rate for the remainder of the semester as a result of not following this regulation). Residents who have pets living with them may also be charged a daily fine, a cleaning charge (to be determined by Facilities) for each area damaged by the animal, and may be billed for the replacement of flooring in areas damaged by their pet.
