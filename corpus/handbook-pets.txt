Quiet Hours: ● Sunday-Thursday 10:00 pm-8:00 am ● Friday & Saturday 12:00 am-10:00 am ● During Finals week there are 24-hour quiet hours that are enforced starting on the day before the ﬁrst day of Finals Sound Equipment : Residents are always expected to keep sound equipment at a level conducive to respecting the rights of others in a community. Stereo speakers must be kept out of windows, and not played in a manner disturbing to the college or wider community. This includes Greek houses, special interest houses, and all residential spaces (including outdoor locations such as the Gazebo). All residents are encouraged to use headphones. Students are not to use their rooms for practicing musical instruments, particularly those that may disturb others. Outdoor Areas Use: Use of the housing adjacent outdoor spaces (Aldrich Field, Chapin Quad, and the 609 Quad) for large-scale events, for example: music performances, inﬂatables, or temporary structure, must be approved by the Director of Residential Life. Persons planning such events must also conform to the Program, Activity, and Event Planning and the Social Host policies. Overnight Guests: Residents may have overnight guests for up to three (3) consecutive nights. As host, you need permission from your roommate, and a guest pass from Security. You also need to notify your RA. All guests must comply with College regulations. Hosts are responsible for the conduct and behavior of their guests. (See Guests of Students ) Pets: No pets, other than ﬁsh in an aquarium (5-gallon tank, maximum), are allowed to live on campus with students. Residents having pets that live with them on campus may be required to have the pet removed from campus and/or be required to move oﬀ-campus (and charged the full room rate for the remainder of the semester as a result of not following this regulation). Residents who have pets living with them may also be charged a daily ﬁne, a cleaning charge (to be determined by Facilities ) for each area damaged by the animal, and may be billed for the replacement of ﬂooring in areas damaged by their pet. At times, oﬀ-campus people from the community, or families of students, will bring pets to campus. Those pets are not allowed in any campus building, nor on the grounds of the Stadium area, and waste must be picked up after them in the outdoor venues. Any charges for damages or cleaning are the responsibility of the pet owner, or the student , if the student is found responsible for the presence of the pet. Note: Emotional Support Animals (as veriﬁed by LEADS) and service animals are not considered pets. 69
