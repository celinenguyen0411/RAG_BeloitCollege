Credit Caps & Grade Minimums:
- Max 15 total transfer units allowed toward the 31-unit degree (grade of "C" or better required).
- Max 8 units allowed from AP, IB, or GCE A-Level exams.
- Max 8 units allowed from non-Beloit online/distance coursework.

Gen Ed & Requirement Restrictions:
- AP, IB, and GCE credits CANNOT satisfy any Domain or Skill requirements.
- Transfer credit can satisfy Quantitative Reasoning ("Q") and 1 Writing ("W") requirement.
- Transfer credit CANNOT satisfy Intercultural Literacy ("C") or the final 2 "W" requirements.
- Earning an Associate of Arts (AA) or Associate of Science (AS) degree automatically satisfies all Domain and Skill requirements.
