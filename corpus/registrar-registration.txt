Registration Process:
- Current Students: Consult with advisor and register online in the semester preceding the term.
- New Students: Register for courses with their assigned advisor.
- Special Students: Register on a space-available basis after degree-seeking students.

Dropping Courses & Deadlines:
- Before Deadline: Dropping a course prior to the official drop deadline removes the course from the record; it does not appear on the transcript.
- After Deadline (Withdraw Passing): Dropping a course after the drop deadline requires petition approval and results in a "WP" notation if passing at time of withdrawal.
- Late Drop: Dropped courses beyond deadline limits without approval result in a grade of "F".
- Full-Time Status Warning: Dropping below full-time status (fewer than 3.0 units) may affect financial aid eligibility, athletic eligibility, campus housing, and visa status for international students.

Waitlist Rules:
- Authorization Required: So long as any student is waitlisted, no student may enroll without instructor authorization (even if enrollment falls below the cap).
- Instructor Approval: Instructors can authorize waitlisted or non-waitlisted students to enroll above cap or waive prerequisites.
- Enrolling via My Beloit: Receiving instructor authorization does NOT automatically enroll you. You must go to the drop/add window in My Beloit to execute the add/drop action.
