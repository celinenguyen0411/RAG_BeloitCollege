The Miller Upton Programs, named for Beloit’s sixth president, bring together leading scholars, young
faculty, and promising students from around the world to examine issues related to increasing the wealth
and well-being of nations. Residing in the college’s economics department, the program has as its
centerpiece “The Wealth and Well-being of Nations: The Miller Upton Forum,” which brings distinguished,
internationally-recognized scholars to campus to work within the classical liberal tradition. The forum unites
faculty, students, and alumni in a consideration of the ideas, institutions, and policy reforms necessary to
promote freedom and prosperity. The program includes a special fund for student-centered intellectual
development and networking, senior year scholarships for talented international students, high-profle
internships for exceptional students, and a fall senior seminar capstone course for all senior economics
majors that focuses on ideas presented in the Upton Forum.
