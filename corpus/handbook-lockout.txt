Food Oversight Committee: This is the primary agent for feedback, evaluation, planning, and communication regarding campus dining services. This committee needs student help and input, and, therefore, invites students to share ideas and thoughts during regularly scheduled meetings. To apply for membership with this committee, reach out to the Beloit Student Government or the SEAL Director. Residential Experience Advisory Committee: REAC operates as a point of contact and conversation between campus community members and the residential and student life staﬀ as well as other campus leadership, as needed. Points of conversation may include service oﬀerings, spending priorities, strategic direction, and compliance concerns. REAC represents various community members by communicating with peers and highlighting ideas for improvements. When needed, REAC may acquire relevant data, invite other administrators to participate, and identify resources to consider the feasibility of improvements and assist in prioritizing the timeline for the proposed changes. Furniture Moving: College property, including furnishings, screens/storms, must not be removed from assigned areas without authorization from a professional Residence Life staﬀ member (not peer Resident Assistants). Students who remove furniture or other items from their designated areas will be billed a per day fee and a per item fee for having the item returned to its original location. A student whose room is missing the original furnishings upon check out will be assessed a charge for the replacement value of these items. Halogen Lamps: New halogen lamps with lamp guards may be acceptable, but should be kept away from window curtains. Incense: Incense is not allowed to be burned on campus, including the residence halls. Keys: At the beginning of the semester, at check-in, each student will be issued a key(s)/key card that will open their room and the exterior entrances to other residential buildings during “open” hours. All students must return their keys at the end of each term. Failure to do so will result in a fee for every key not turned in. Keys may not be duplicated. See below (“Lost”) for lost key/card charges. Laundry: Clothes washers and dryers are located in the basement of most residential facilities. Problems with the equipment should be reported directly to the Wash company at 1-424-757-9195, or through their website, wash.com . Please note that all maintenance by the company will occur during Facilities oﬃce hours. There is no cost to the use of the washers and dryers. Lock-Out Policy: Residents are expected to carry room and building keys (and IDs) with them at all times. Locked doors and attention to keys are essential to students’ personal safety and the safety of their property. In the event a student is locked out of their room during a college work day, the student should call the Residence Life Oﬃce (608-363-2350). If it is after oﬃce hours or on the weekend, the student should contact Security, who can let them in. If the student loses their key, they should contact Residence Life immediately, ﬁll out a key replacement form, and take it to Security to have a new key cut for their room. 67
