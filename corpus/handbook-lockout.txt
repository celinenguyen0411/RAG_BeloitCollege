Residents are expected to carry room and building keys (and IDs) with them at all times. Locked doors and attention to keys are essential to students' personal safety and the safety of their property. In the event a student is locked out of their room during a college work day, the student should call the Residence Life Office (608-363-2350). If it is after office hours or on the weekend, the student should contact Security, who can let them in.
