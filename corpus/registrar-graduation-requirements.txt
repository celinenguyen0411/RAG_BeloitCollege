General Graduation Criteria:
- Total Units & GPA: Must complete at least 31 units of earned credit with a cumulative GPA of at least 2.000, and a major GPA of at least 2.000.
- Major & Capstone: Completion of a departmental/interdisciplinary major and a Capstone experience (0.5 or 1 unit, taken after the 4th semester).
- Catalog Choice: Students may choose degree requirements from the catalog at entrance, major declaration, or graduation.

Skills & Liberal Arts Breadth (Domains):
- Skills Requirements: Must complete Writing ("W"), Quantitative Reasoning ("Q"), and Intercultural Literacy ("C") across 5 different courses.
- Domain Requirements: Must complete 5 Liberal Arts Breadth Domains (1S, 2A, 3B, 4U, 5T) normally by the end of the 4th semester. Each domain must use a different course prefix (e.g., PSYC, BIOL, HIST).
- AP/IB Policy Restriction: AP, IB, and GCE A-level credits CANNOT be used to satisfy Skills (W, Q, C) or Domain requirements.

Experience (E) & Capstone Rules:
- Experience Requirement (E): Must complete at least 1 unit equivalent via E-courses, research, internship, study abroad, or synthesized experiences.
- Double-Counting Rule: A single course/unit CANNOT simultaneously satisfy both the Capstone requirement and the Experience requirement.
