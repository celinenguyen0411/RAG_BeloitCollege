### Direct Questions
Question 1: Under the repeat policy applicable from Fall 2023 onward, can I repeat a course in which I earned a D+, and which attempt counts toward my GPA and earned units?
Expected answer: A D+ is eligible. The most recent attempt counts toward GPA and earned units; the earlier attempt receives zero credit and is excluded from GPA calculation. Repeating requires instructor approval and a signed add/drop card, and space is not guaranteed. The policy lists exceptions for certain course types

Question 2: What three academic progress standards determine continued financial-aid eligibility?
Expected answer: Cumulative GPA of at least 2.0; successful completion of at least 67% of attempted units; and a maximum timeframe rule under which eligibility ends once attempted units reach 150% or more of the degree requirement.

Question 3: How many units can be double counted if a student majoring in Data Science or Data Analytics pursues another major or minor?
Expected answer: Up to four (4) units are allowed to be double counted toward another major or minor.

Question 4: What initial steps must a student take to resolve a roommate or floor mate conflict before a room change can be approved?
Expected answer: The process begins by speaking directly with your roommate or floor mate. If the conflict cannot be worked out, you must then speak with your Resident Assistant (RA) to facilitate mediation. All room changes must ultimately be approved by the Residence Life Office.

### Multi-Document Questions
Question 5: What is Beloit's normal course load, and how many units ordinarily count as full-time enrollment?
Expected answer: The normal load is 4.00-4.25 units. Ordinarily, full-time enrollment requires at least 3 units; the policy notes an exception with advisor and registrar approval.

Question 6: Can a student use Advanced Placement (AP) credit to fulfill the Quantitative Reasoning ("Q") or Writing ("W") general education skills requirements for graduation?
Expected answer: No. AP credit cannot satisfy any Domain or Skill requirements (including "Q" and "W"). While standard transfer credit from an accredited college can satisfy "Q" and 1 "W", AP credits are strictly restricted from satisfying these requirements.

Question 7: How do the capstone requirements and off-campus/transfer unit allowances compare between the Economics major and the Data Science major?
Expected answer: Economics requires a Senior Capstone (ECON 380) or Senior Thesis (ECON 390) and allows a maximum of 2 transfer units toward the major. Data Science requires DSDA 310 (.5 unit) and allows up to 4 units to be double counted toward another major or minor.

### Conversational Questions
Question 8: I missed the drop deadline, but I'm still getting a D. Can I get out of the class without it just becoming an F?
Expected answer: A withdraw-passing petition may be an option, not an automatic approval. It requires a written petition, an instructor statement confirming a D or better, and an advisor statement. Submit by the semester's last day of classes. If approved, WP appears on the transcript and earns no credit.

Question 9: I locked myself out of my room on a Saturday night at 11 PM. Who am I supposed to call to get back in?
Expected answer: Since it is after office hours and on the weekend, you should contact Security to be let back into your room. During standard workday office hours, you would call the Residence Life Office at 608-363-2350.
